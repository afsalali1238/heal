## 2. Alt text — five rows below the 45-character floor

`validate.ts` rule `alt-text-too-thin`. The floor exists because alt text is read aloud to a
patient using a screen reader, and it has to let them **do the exercise from the description
alone** — so it must describe the position, not name the movement. `compliance.ts` deliberately
does _not_ exempt `image_alt_en` from the banned-term scan for the same reason.

**The drafts below are drafts.** They are assembled from the clinician's own
`start_position_en` and `movement_en` wording for that same row — nothing new was invented — but
describing a body position is clinical content, so **every one needs her review and edit before it
goes in the sheet.** Ship them as her words, not these.

## 3. Not blocking the build, but blocking launch

These are warnings, not errors. They do not stop a deploy; they stop the site being finished.

**`reviewed_by` is empty on all 26 items, including the 5 published ones.**
Rule `unreviewed-published-item`. No clinician has signed off on anything a patient can currently
read. This needs a decision, not just a fix:

- If she signs off, fill `reviewed_by` and `reviewed_date` in the sheet.
- If unsigned content should never be publishable, promote the rule from `warn` to `error` in
  `src/lib/validate.ts` and the build will refuse. That is a one-line change and it is the honest
  one — right now the rule fires on every build and nothing happens.

**`image_status` is unset on all 26 items.** Patients see text-only cards. `IMAGE-PIPELINE.md`
says only `approved` ships, and `ExerciseImage` enforces it, so this is correct behaviour — but it
means the library has no pictures at all until she reviews each image against its instruction text
and sets `approved`.

**`src/config/clinic.ts` has 7 unfilled values** — legal name, DHA facility licence,
physiotherapist name and licence, medical director, phone, last content review date. These were
deliberately left as `TO BE SUPPLIED` rather than guessed. The footer shows each as "not yet
supplied" in the meantime. Set `COMPLIANCE_STRICT=1` in the Vercel environment and the build will
refuse to ship with them blank.

**The three legal documents have `approvedBy: null`** — disclaimer, privacy, credits. The clinic's
Medical Director is accountable for content approval (RESEARCH-FINDINGS §4).
